# USB Charge Control v0.8.11

Appearance-only update from v0.8.10 for Dopamine rootless iOS 16.

- Highlights the **Input Rail mA value** permanently in green so the main current reading stands out.
- Leaves the **Input Rail label and W reading white** and keeps every other readout and control unchanged.
- Green applies even when no reading is available (the `— mA` placeholder).

**Unchanged:** Charge Control home-screen name, compact Charging Data layout, charging/USB voltage combined line, USB cap controls, restore, daemon, telemetry delay and background watchdog.

Install directly over v0.8.10 through Sileo.
